Frequently asked questions

General & services

What is a porter service for seniors?

A porter service fills the gap between home maintenance and caregiving. Home health aides handle personal and medical care, like bathing, meals and medications. Handymen and contractors handle bigger repairs and renovations. A porter takes care of everything in between: light chores, safety hazards, grocery and errand runs, clearing walkways and routine home upkeep.

It gives seniors one reliable person to call so they can keep living independently at home, and it gives families a helping hand when they can’t be there themselves.

My parent doesn’t need a medical caregiver, just help around the house. Is this a good fit?

Yes, that’s exactly who Jason’s Porter Service is for. Many older adults are independent day to day but struggle with ladders, heavy lifting, snow removal or driving. Jason handles those physical jobs so your parent can age in place safely, comfortably and on their own terms.

What does it cost?

There are two ways to work with Jason. The Porter Plan is $200 per month and covers weekly chores, errands, one appointment ride, seasonal upkeep and as-needed fixes like light bulbs and snow removal. You can cancel anytime with no fees.

If you’d rather not subscribe, you can book single jobs à la carte. Those are quoted per job, so you’ll know the price before any work starts.

Can we book just one job, like a gutter cleaning?

Yes. Any service in the Porter Plan can be booked on its own, along with extras like additional appointment rides, wellness checks and help moving or selling belongings. Each job is quoted separately.

What if we need something that isn’t on your list?

Just ask. If it helps your parent stay safe and comfortable at home, there’s a good chance Jason can help or point you to someone who can. Send a note or call or text 720-468-0906.

Emergencies

Do you help with household emergencies?

Yes. Jason offers on-call help for urgent home problems such as burst pipes, leaks and similar issues, so your parent isn’t left waiting alone while you try to reach a plumber from across town.

How do we reach Jason in an emergency?

Call or text Jason directly at 720-468-0906. For anything urgent, a call is faster than the contact form.

Is Jason a substitute for 911 or a medical caregiver?

No. For a fall, injury or any medical emergency, call 911 first. Jason is CPR certified, but he is not a medical caregiver and doesn’t provide personal or medical care. Once help is on the way, he can be there to support your parent and keep the family informed.

Trust, security & discretion

Can you do wellness spot-checks or check on other home care providers?

Yes. Families who live across town or out of state often worry about whether hired caregivers are showing up and doing their job. Jason can stop by for scheduled or unannounced wellness spot-checks to make sure caregivers are present, doing their duties and treating your parent and their home with respect.

If you’re concerned about belongings going missing, he can also do a possessions check and, with your family’s approval, install cameras.

Will you sign a non-disclosure or confidentiality agreement?

Absolutely. Many homes hold family heirlooms, valuables, collectibles or private records. Discretion is central to how Jason works, and he’s happy to sign a family NDA or a custom confidentiality agreement before service begins.

How do family updates and kin-approved billing work?

After visits, designated family members get clear updates, and every Porter Plan includes monthly status reports and weekly progress photos.

Billing is approved by an adult child or trustee rather than the senior alone. That keeps everyone on the same page, protects parents on a limited or fixed income from unexpected charges, and takes the paperwork off their plate.

Real estate & future planning

Why does it help that Jason is also a licensed Realtor?

Through regular maintenance and visits, Jason gets to know a home’s layout, systems and upkeep history in detail. When it’s time to downsize, move to senior living or settle an estate, your family doesn’t have to bring in a stranger who spends weeks learning the property. The home’s history is already known, trust is already built, and the house can go to market with no learning curve.

How does this help families selling a parent’s home?

Selling a parent’s home is often an emotional, stressful time. Working with your porter as your listing agent offers a few clear advantages:

  • Lower listing fee. Jason lists at 2.5% instead of the typical 3.2% in Colorado. On an average home, that keeps thousands of dollars with your family or the estate.
  • A plan to sell quickly. Utilities, insurance on a vacant home and HOA dues add up while a house sits. Accurate, competitive pricing attracts serious buyers early and helps you close cleanly.
  • A known maintenance history. Knowing what was serviced, repaired and improved makes seller disclosures simpler, pricing more accurate and buyer negotiations more confident.
  • A gentle, organized process. From pre-listing repairs and staging to showings, estate sales and moving belongings, one trusted person coordinates it all with care.
